# Zero Manual — Pitch Deck (Final)

11 slides. YC seed structure. Awareness deck, no Ask/Financials/Traction.

---

## Slide 1 — Title

**Zero Manual**

**The Self-Driving Company Brain**

Gives AI agents and tools the company understanding they need to actually operate.

AI is becoming part of every company.

Now every company needs a Brain.

---

## Slide 2 — Problem

Give an AI agent access to your company tools today. It reads documents, messages, tickets, databases.

But access is not understanding.

It still doesn't know: why decisions were made, who owns what, what was promised, what changed over time.

Ask your best salesperson why a deal fell apart eight months ago — they'll know. Ask your CRM. It won't.

Because the most valuable company knowledge was never stored.

It lived in people.

Every AI interaction starts from zero. Every agent re-learns context a human already has.

---

## Slide 3 — Why Now

AI is moving from answering questions to performing work.

Agents write code, talk to customers, analyze information, execute workflows.

But every worker needs context before they can create value — AI workers are no different.

Agents only act as well as the understanding they receive.

The bottleneck isn't model intelligence anymore.

It's organizational understanding.

Y Combinator named this exact gap a priority category this year: **Company Brain.**

---

## Slide 4 — Solution

**Zero Manual is the Self-Driving Company Brain.**

It continuously turns company activity — conversations, meetings, decisions, work — into living organizational understanding.

Then delivers that understanding wherever work happens: AI agents, tools, and teams.

So when someone or something asks:

* *Why did we choose this vendor?*
* *What did we promise this customer?*
* *Has this problem happened before?*
* *What should this agent know before acting?*

The company already understands the answer.

Knowledge stops disappearing.

Context becomes infrastructure.

---

## Slide 5 — How It Delivers (priority order)

**1. Context for AI Agents — the core product.**

Claude, ChatGPT, internal agents, developer agents.

Before acting, every agent gets the company understanding it needs.

Real operational context:

* past decisions
* ownership
* history
* constraints
* commitments

Not a document dump.

A working memory of the company.

---

**2. Context Inside Existing Tools.**

Slack, Gmail, CRM.

Tools become aware of company context.

The right knowledge appears where work already happens.

---
**3. Company Brain Platform.**

A dedicated interface to understand and interact with your organization.

See what's changing, what needs attention, where risks exist, and where knowledge gaps are forming.

Includes an AI Consultant that helps teams ask questions, analyze situations, review decisions, and understand what is happening across the company.

Lowest priority surface — agent and tool layers are where the product starts.

The Brain matters most when it powers work. The platform exists to help humans understand and collaborate with it.

## Slide 7 — What Makes This a New Category

Search finds information.

Company Brain creates understanding.

| Existing Systems | Zero Manual            |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| Documents        | Organizational reality |
| Search            | Understanding          |
| Static knowledge | Living memory          |
| Retrieval        | Trust + governance     |

Other teams moving toward this category validates the need.

Most stop at capture and retrieval.

A real Company Brain needs to know:

* where knowledge came from
* whether it's still true
* who owns it
* whether an agent can safely act on it

The future isn't better search.

It's companies that understand themselves.
