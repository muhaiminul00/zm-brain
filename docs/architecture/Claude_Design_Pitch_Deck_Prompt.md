# Claude Design — Prompt: Zero Manual Pitch Deck

Build an 11-slide pitch deck for Zero Manual. Apply ZeroManual brand guidelines exactly — dark-first, orange accent, Space Grotesk headings, Inter body. Awareness/category-introduction deck, not a fundraising ask deck. No animations needed, static slides, presentation-ready, 16:9.

## Brand system — apply to every slide, no exceptions

**Colors**
- Background: `#0D1117` (default canvas, every slide)
- Surface/cards: `#161B22`, hover/raised: `#1C2128`
- Borders: `#30363D`
- Navy accents: `#1A2B3C`
- Orange (primary accent): `#FF6B00` — hover/alt: `#E05A00`, subtle tint: `#FF6B001A`
- Text primary: `#F0F6FC`
- Text secondary: `#8B949E`
- Text muted: `#484F58`

**Typography**
- Headings: Space Grotesk, 700 weight, never italic, tight letter-spacing (-0.02em)
- Body: Inter, 400/500 weight, line-height 1.6–1.75
- Code/technical labels only (if any): JetBrains Mono
- Display/hero text: 48–64px · H1: 36px · H2: 28px · H3: 22px · Body large: 18px · Body: 16px

**Rules — do not violate**
- Dark background only, every slide, no exceptions, no light slides
- Exactly one prominent orange element per slide — never more
- No gradients, no heavy shadows/glow, no illustrations or stock-photo style
- Border radius: 6px (buttons/inputs), 8px (cards) — nothing above 12px
- Generous whitespace — 64–96px between major sections, don't cram
- Sharp, structured, confident — not decorative or "startup-cute"

## Deck-wide direction

- One idea per slide, large type, readable from the back of a room (YC design standard)
- Slide 5 is the emotional center of the deck — give it the most visual weight and space of any slide. Show three tiers stacked vertically or as three horizontal bands, sized to reflect priority: AI Agents band largest/boldest, Tools band medium, Platform band smallest/quietest. Use orange only on the Agents tier to draw the eye there first.
- Slide 6 ("How It Would Look") should read as a clear before/after split-screen or two-column layout — left column muted/grey tone (the "Today" pain), right column normal brand color with orange checkmarks (the "With Zero Manual" outcome). This is illustrative UI only — stylized mockup treatment, not a literal screenshot, not claiming to be a live product.
- Slide 7's comparison table should be genuinely visual, not a plain markdown table pasted in — styled as a two-column card comparison with a clear divider, left column muted/grey, right column in brand text-primary with the orange accent on the "Zero Manual" column header only.
- Slide 9 (Team) — six names in a clean grid (3x2 or 2x3), name + role only, consistent card treatment per person, no photos needed (use simple avatar placeholders — initials on a navy circle is fine, on-brand).
- Title slide (1) and closing slide (11) should feel like bookends — same layout language, closing slide can slightly increase type size on the final line ("Tomorrow companies become intelligent") as the emotional peak of the whole deck.

## Slide-by-slide content

Use the exact copy below verbatim — do not rewrite, condense, or add marketing flourishes beyond what's here. Formatting/emphasis (bold, checkmarks, quote-style questions) should carry into the visual design as indicated.

---

**Slide 1 — Title**
- Zero Manual
- The Self-Driving Company Brain
- Gives AI agents and tools the company understanding they need to actually operate.
- AI is becoming part of every company.
- Now every company needs a Brain.

---

**Slide 2 — Problem**
- Give an AI agent access to your company tools today. It reads documents, messages, tickets, databases.
- But access is not understanding.
- It still doesn't know: why decisions were made, who owns what, what was promised, what changed over time.
- Ask your best salesperson why a deal fell apart eight months ago — they'll know. Ask your CRM. It won't.
- Because the most valuable company knowledge was never stored. It lived in people.
- Every AI interaction starts from zero. Every agent re-learns context a human already has.

---

**Slide 3 — Why Now**
- AI is moving from answering questions to performing work.
- Agents write code, talk to customers, analyze information, execute workflows.
- But every worker needs context before they can create value — AI workers are no different.
- Agents only act as well as the understanding they receive.
- The bottleneck isn't model intelligence anymore. It's organizational understanding.
- Y Combinator named this exact gap a priority category this year: **Company Brain.** (treat this line as a callout/highlight — small badge or accent-bordered box, using orange sparingly)

---

**Slide 4 — Solution**
- Zero Manual is the Self-Driving Company Brain.
- It continuously turns company activity — conversations, meetings, decisions, work — into living organizational understanding.
- Then delivers that understanding wherever work happens: AI agents, tools, and teams.
- So when someone or something asks:
  - "Why did we choose this vendor?"
  - "What did we promise this customer?"
  - "Has this problem happened before?"
  - "What should this agent know before acting?"
- The company already understands the answer.
- Knowledge stops disappearing. Context becomes infrastructure.
(Style the four questions as a stacked list of quote-card chips — subtle surface background, left-aligned, consistent spacing.)

---

**Slide 5 — How It Delivers (priority order)**

*Tier 1 — largest, boldest, orange accent:*
Context for AI Agents — the core product.
Claude, ChatGPT, internal agents, developer agents. Before acting, every agent gets the company understanding it needs. Real operational context: past decisions, ownership, history, constraints, commitments. Not a document dump — a working memory of the company.

*Tier 2 — medium weight, navy/surface tone:*
Context Inside Existing Tools.
Slack, Gmail, CRM. Tools become aware of company context. The right knowledge appears where work already happens.

*Tier 3 — smallest, quietest, muted tone:*
Company Brain Platform.
A dedicated interface to understand and interact with your organization. See what's changing, what needs attention, where risks exist, and where knowledge gaps are forming. Includes an AI Consultant that helps teams ask questions, analyze situations, review decisions, and understand what is happening across the company. Lowest priority surface — agent and tool layers are where the product starts. The Brain matters most when it powers work; the platform exists to help humans understand and collaborate with it.

---

**Slide 6 — How It Would Look**

*Left column ("Today") — muted/grey tone:*
AI Agent: "Please upload documents and explain the background."
Human: Repeats context. Again. Again.

*Right column ("With Zero Manual") — brand tone, orange checkmarks:*
AI Agent: "Create a launch plan."
Already has:
✓ previous launch decisions
✓ team responsibilities
✓ current constraints
✓ historical mistakes
✓ relevant customer insights
✓ lessons learned
AI doesn't start with a blank conversation. It starts with company understanding.

---

**Slide 7 — What Makes This a New Category**
- Search finds information. Company Brain creates understanding.
- Comparison (Existing Systems → Zero Manual):
  - Documents → Organizational reality
  - Search → Understanding
  - Static knowledge → Living memory
  - Retrieval → Trust + governance
- Other teams moving toward this category validates the need. Most stop at capture and retrieval.
- A real Company Brain needs to know: where knowledge came from, whether it's still true, who owns it, whether an agent can safely act on it.
- The future isn't better search. It's companies that understand themselves.

---

**Slide 8 — Market**
- Every platform era created a new system of record.
  - CRM → customer memory
  - ERP → operational memory
  - HR systems → employee memory
  - **AI era → Company Brain: organizational memory** (highlight this row, orange accent)
- The missing system is the one that understands the company itself.
- Path: AI-native teams → Startups → Enterprises → Every knowledge organization (render as a horizontal arrow/funnel progression)
- As AI becomes a worker inside companies, every company will need a Brain.
